# Lockfile Ofrenda

## Register
product

## Platform
web

## Purpose
Show a developer which packages in *their* npm lockfile have gone quiet, then write a short remembrance from registry facts only.

## Users
Someone who can drop a `package-lock.json` and wants to know what they still depend on that the registry has already forgotten.

## Positioning
Not a haunted terminal and not a package.json-only health scanner. The lockfile is the ofrenda: every transitive ghost counts. The model may not invent CVEs, successors, or maintainers.

## Personality
Municipal death register meets Día de los Muertos. Precise, a little wry, never cruel.

## Anti-references
Generic Halloween purple neon, SaaS metric cards, glassmorphism dashboards, ChatGPT-in-a-hoodie.

## Accessibility
Body text contrast ≥ 7:1, visible focus, keyboard for drop zone and ledger, `prefers-reduced-motion`, eulogy announced with `aria-live`.
